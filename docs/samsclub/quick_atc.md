This method allows you to quickly add items to your cart on Sam's Club, ensuring success even when the site is extremely slow or under heavy load.

Instructions
1. Add the item to your list
Navigate to the product page of the item you want and add it to your personal list.

2. Go to your lists
Once added, head over to the lists page you just created: https://www.samsclub.com/lists

3. Open the Console
You will need to open your browser's Developer Console.

Windows / ChromeOS / Linux: Press Ctrl + Shift + J (or press F12 and navigate to the "Console" tab)
Mac: Press Cmd + Option + J
